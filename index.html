Filmaholic

Film look camera and darkroom

Filmaholic shoots with film character built in — halation, grain, film colour and print stages, live in the viewfinder — and has a darkroom for giving any photo a film look.

Help

How do I change film?
Tap the film box at the top left of the camera. Pick a film to preview it live, then tap Done.
Where do my photos go?
Straight into your Photos library. Tap Photos beside the shutter to open it, or Darkroom to edit your last shot.
Can I edit photos I already have?
Yes. Open the darkroom, tap Photo and choose one, RAW files included. The proof sheet shows every look on your own picture.
Skin looks too orange.
Tap the face button at the top right of the camera to turn on skin protection, or use the Skin protection slider in the darkroom's Colour tab.
What's RAW?
On iPhone Pro models, Filmaholic shoots Apple ProRAW and develops it itself, so highlights roll off like film instead of clipping. Other iPhones shoot a processed photo, which works with every look too.
Changing 9:16 and 4:3
Use the ratio button next to the flip-camera button.
How do I delete my community account?
Open the community, tap your avatar, then Delete my community account. It deletes your username and everything you've published, rated or entered. Or email us and we'll do it for you.
How do I report something in the community?
Open it and tap Report, or Block to hide everything from that person. See the community guidelines.
