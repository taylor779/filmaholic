Filmaholic

Community guidelines

Effective 9 October 2026 · Below Studios

Community stocks are film looks that people make in Filmaholic and publish for everyone else to use. Each one goes up with the photo it was made on, so you can see what it does before you add it.

When you publish

Stocks aren't reviewed before they appear. When you publish, you confirm that:

  • the look is yours to share — you made it, or you have the right to give it away;
  • the sample photo is yours, and anyone recognisable in it is happy to be in it;
  • you take responsibility for what you publish.

What isn't allowed

  • Nudity, sexual content, violence or gore in the sample photo.
  • Hate, harassment, or anything aimed at a person or group.
  • Someone else's paid presets or LUTs, or looks named after another company's products.
  • Spam, advertising, or names and photos meant to mislead.
  • Personal information, yours or anyone else's, in a name or photo.

Reporting and blocking

Open any stock and tap Report. It's hidden for you straight away, and a stock that several people report stops being listed for everyone while we look at it. Block hides everything from that publisher on your iPhone. We review reports and remove stocks, and the accounts that publish them, that break these guidelines.

Removing your stock

Open it in the community and tap Delete from the community. People who already added it keep their copy.
