Filmaholic

Privacy policy

Effective 8 October 2026 · Below Studios

Filmaholic doesn't collect any information about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app doesn't connect to the internet. Everything it does happens on your iPhone.

What the app uses, and why

Camera
To show your film look live in the viewfinder and to take photos and videos. Frames are processed on your iPhone and are never sent anywhere.
Microphone
Only in video mode, to record sound with your videos.
Adding to Photos
To save the photos and videos you take or export. Filmaholic only asks to add to your library; it can't see what's already there.
Photos you choose
When you open a photo in the darkroom, you pick it with Apple's own photo picker, and Filmaholic only receives the photo you chose.

What stays on your iPhone

Your settings and any looks you save are stored on your iPhone. While you're using the app, it keeps working copies of photos you've taken or opened so the darkroom can edit them; iOS clears these out. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Sharing

We don't receive your data, so we have nothing to share or sell. Photos only leave your iPhone if you share or sync them yourself, for example through iCloud Photos, under Apple's terms.

Children

Filmaholic is suitable for everyone and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
